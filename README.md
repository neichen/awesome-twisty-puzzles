# Awesome Twisty Puzzles

A curated list of websites, tools, and learning resources for speedcubing and other twisty puzzles.

This list is a starting point for people who want to learn a method, time solves, look up algorithms, use a solver or simulator, or read about the mathematics behind the puzzles. It is not limited to the 3×3 cube.

This list started from [awesome-rubiks-cube](https://github.com/nirajpandkar/awesome-rubiks-cube) by [Niraj Pandkar](https://github.com/nirajpandkar), released under [CC0 1.0](LICENSE). Entries from that list were rechecked; dead or parked links were dropped, moved URLs were updated, and the list was expanded.

**Disclosure:** The maintainer of this list is the author of [Cube Solver Lab](https://cube-solver.org/), which is listed under [Solvers](#solvers).

## Official & Competitions

- [World Cube Association](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/) - Organization that governs official speedcubing competitions and publishes results.
- [WCA Competitions](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/competitions) - Calendar of upcoming and past official competitions.
- [WCA Live](https://live.worldcubeassociation.org/) - Official site for entering and following live competition results.
- [WCA Regulations](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/regulations/) - Official rules and guidelines for WCA competitions.
- [WCA Results Export](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/export/results) - Public export of the official WCA results database.
- [Cubing.com (粗饼)](https://cubing.com/) - Chinese cubing competition website, with contest listings and results.
- [Rubik's](https://rubiks.com/) - Official website of the Rubik's brand, including beginner solution guides.

## Learning & Tutorials

- [CFOP Discussion](https://www.speedsolving.com/threads/cfop-discussion.6085/) - Long-running SpeedSolving.com help thread on improving with the CFOP method.
- [CFOP method (wiki)](https://www.speedsolving.com/wiki/index.php/CFOP) - Speedsolving Wiki article on the CFOP (Fridrich) method.
- [CubeMastery](https://cubemastery.com/rubiks-cube-tutorial-step-by-step/) - Step-by-step beginner guide to solving a 3×3 cube.
- [CubeSkills](https://www.cubeskills.com/) - Tutorials and algorithm resources from Feliks Zemdegs.
- [How to Solve a Rubik's Cube (Part One)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HsQIoPyfQzM) - Dan Brown's beginner video tutorial.
- [J Perm](https://jperm.net/) - Speedcubing tutorials and algorithm sheets.
- [Petrus method (wiki)](https://www.speedsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Petrus_Method) - Speedsolving Wiki article on the Petrus method.
- [Roux method (wiki)](https://www.speedsolving.com/wiki/index.php/Roux_Method) - Speedsolving Wiki article on the Roux method.
- [Rubik's 3×3 Solve Guide](https://rubiks.com/solve-guide) - Official beginner solution guide for the 3×3 cube.
- [Ruwix](https://ruwix.com/) - Reference site for puzzle notation, types, and solution methods.
- [Ruwix Beginner's Method](https://ruwix.com/the-rubiks-cube/how-to-solve-the-rubiks-cube-beginners-method/) - Layer-by-layer beginner's method tutorial.
- [Ruwix CFOP](https://ruwix.com/the-rubiks-cube/advanced-cfop-fridrich/) - Overview of the CFOP (Fridrich) method.
- [Speedsolving Wiki](https://www.speedsolving.com/wiki/) - Community encyclopedia of methods, algorithms, events, and terminology.
- [ZZ method (wiki)](https://www.speedsolving.com/wiki/index.php/ZZ_Method) - Speedsolving Wiki article on the ZZ method.

## Algorithms & Reconstruction

- [333.fm](https://333.fm/) - Fewest-moves practice site with an insertion finder (same project as fewestmov.es).
- [CubeDB](https://cubedb.net/) - Algorithm database and collection of solve reconstructions.
- [Nissy](https://nissy.tronto.net/) - Cube solver and fewest-moves assistant.
- [SpeedCubeDB](https://speedcubedb.com/) - Searchable algorithm database for many WCA events and steps.

## Timers

- [csTimer](https://cstimer.net/) - Browser timer for WCA events, with statistics, session tools, and optional hardware input.
- [CubeDesk](https://www.cubedesk.io/) - Browser timer with training features and online matches.
- [CubeTimer](https://cubetimer.com/) - Simple online cube timer.
- [CubingTime](https://cubingtime.com/timer) - Online speedcubing timer with contest features.
- [qqTimer](https://qqtimer.net/) - Lightweight keyboard-driven browser timer.
- [Ruwix CubeTimer](https://ruwix.com/online-rubiks-stopwatch-timer/) - Online cube timer with a performance graph.

## Solvers

- [Cube Explorer](https://kociemba.org/cube.htm) - Herbert Kociemba's program for solving and analyzing the 3×3 cube with the two-phase algorithm.
- [Cube Solver Lab](https://cube-solver.org/) - Open-source browser solver for 2×2–15×15 cubes, Pyraminx, Skewb and Megaminx, with 3D step-by-step playback and a permutation graph.
- [Ruwix Cube Solver](https://ruwix.com/cube-solver/) - Online 3×3 cube solver.

## Simulators & Virtual Cubes

- [alg.cubing.net](https://alg.cubing.net/) - Algorithm and reconstruction viewer and virtual cube player.
- [AnimCubeJS](https://animcubejs.cubing.net/) - Browser cube animation simulator, ported from Josef Jelinek's AnimCube.
- [cubing.net](https://www.cubing.net/) - Directory of cubing.js, Twizzle, and related tools.
- [Ruwix Simulators](https://ruwix.com/simulators/) - Browser simulators for the 3×3 cube and other twisty puzzles.
- [Twizzle](https://alpha.twizzle.net/) - Puzzle player, algorithm editor, and reconstruction viewer built on cubing.js.
- [VisualCube](https://cube.rider.biz/visualcube.php) - Image API for generating cube diagrams (Conrad Rider).

## Software Libraries

- [cubing.js](https://js.cubing.net/cubing/) - JavaScript library for puzzle geometry, algorithm notation, scrambling, and the `<twisty-player>` viewer. Source: [cubing/cubing.js](https://github.com/cubing/cubing.js).
- [gullicube](https://github.com/rokicki/gullicube) - Tomas Rokicki's general solver for large NxN cubes.
- [min2phase](https://github.com/cs0x7f/min2phase) - Optimized implementation of Kociemba's two-phase algorithm, used by several timers and scramblers.
- [RubiksCube-TwophaseSolver](https://github.com/hkociemba/RubiksCube-TwophaseSolver) - Herbert Kociemba's Python two-phase solver.
- [TNoodle](https://github.com/thewca/tnoodle) - Official WCA scramble program.
- [twips](https://github.com/cubing/twips) - Twizzle Pattern Searcher, a program for finding algorithms and scrambles on twisty puzzles (formerly twsearch).

## Math & Theory

- [God's Number is 20](https://cube20.org/) - Background on the proof that every 3×3 position can be solved in at most 20 moves in the half-turn metric.
- [Jaap's Puzzle Page](https://www.jaapsch.net/puzzles/) - Encyclopedia of twisty and mechanical puzzles, with solutions and mathematical notes.
- [Kociemba's Homepage](https://kociemba.org/) - Herbert Kociemba's site, including Cube Explorer and a description of the two-phase algorithm.
- [The Two-Phase Algorithm](https://kociemba.org/twophase.htm) - Kociemba's write-up of the two-phase 3×3 solving algorithm.
